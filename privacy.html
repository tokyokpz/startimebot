Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information StarTime ("the Bot") may process, how that information is used, and the choices available to users.

1. Information We Process

Discord Information

Depending on the features being used, StarTime may process information provided through Discord, including:

  • Discord User ID
  • Discord username or display name
  • Discord Server ID
  • Discord channel or voice-channel information
  • Roles and permissions necessary for Bot functionality

Voice Activity Data

If voice tracking features are enabled, StarTime may record information about participation in Discord voice channels, such as:

  • When a user joins a voice channel
  • When a user leaves a voice channel
  • The amount of time spent in voice channels
  • Total accumulated voice time used for statistics or leaderboards

StarTime does not record, store, or listen to the contents of voice conversations.

Command and Usage Data

StarTime may process information about commands and interactions when necessary to operate features, troubleshoot problems, prevent abuse, or maintain the service.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by StarTime may be used to:

  • Provide and operate Bot features
  • Calculate voice-time statistics
  • Generate leaderboards
  • Identify users within a Discord server
  • Maintain server-specific settings
  • Troubleshoot technical problems
  • Prevent abuse or misuse
  • Improve StarTime and its features

We do not sell users' personal information.

3. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

StarTime does not intentionally collect:

  • Discord passwords
  • Payment card information
  • Private messages unrelated to Bot functionality
  • Voice conversation content
  • Audio recordings of voice channels

4. Data Storage

Information processed by StarTime may be stored in databases or other systems used to operate the Bot.

The type and amount of stored information may vary depending on the features enabled by a Discord server.

Reasonable measures are used to protect stored information from unauthorized access. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent users' personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party infrastructure or service providers when necessary to operate StarTime, such as hosting, database, or logging providers.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, protect the security of StarTime or its users, or investigate abuse, fraud, or unauthorized access.

6. Server Administrators

Server administrators control whether StarTime is installed on their server and which features are enabled.

If you do not want your activity processed by StarTime, you may leave a server that uses the Bot or contact the relevant server administrator.

7. Data Deletion Requests

Users may request deletion of data associated with their Discord account where technically possible.

To request deletion, contact the StarTime developer through the official StarTime support or community channel.

You may be asked to provide your Discord User ID so that the correct data can be identified.

Certain information may need to be retained for legitimate technical, security, or legal purposes.

8. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide StarTime's features, maintain statistics, prevent abuse, resolve technical issues, or meet legal obligations.

Server-specific data may be deleted when StarTime is removed from a server, depending on the type of data and how the Bot is configured.

9. Children's Privacy

StarTime is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

Users should not provide sensitive personal information to StarTime through commands or other interactions.

10. Third-Party Services

StarTime operates through Discord and may use third-party hosting, database, or other infrastructure providers.

Those services may process information necessary for StarTime to operate and may have their own terms and privacy policies.

Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own policies.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as StarTime's features or data practices change.

The "Last updated" date will be changed when updates are made.

12. Contact

For questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, contact the StarTime developer through the official StarTime support or community channel.
