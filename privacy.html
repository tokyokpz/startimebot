Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Tokyo After Dark Discord Bot ("the Bot") may collect, how that information is used, and the choices available to users.

1. Information We Collect

Discord Information

The Bot may receive information provided by Discord, such as:

  • Discord User ID
  • Discord username or display name
  • Discord Server ID
  • Discord channel or voice-channel information
  • Discord roles and permissions necessary for Bot functionality

Voice Activity Data

If voice tracking features are enabled in a server, the Bot may record information related to a user's participation in Discord voice channels, such as:

  • When a user joins a voice channel
  • When a user leaves a voice channel
  • The amount of time spent in voice channels
  • Accumulated voice time used for statistics or leaderboards

The Bot does not record or store the contents of voice conversations.

Command and Usage Data

The Bot may process information about commands and interactions with the Bot when necessary to operate its features, troubleshoot problems, prevent abuse, or maintain the service.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by the Bot may be used to:

  • Provide and operate Bot features
  • Calculate voice-time statistics
  • Generate leaderboards
  • Identify Discord users within a server
  • Maintain server-specific settings
  • Troubleshoot errors and technical problems
  • Prevent abuse or misuse of the Bot
  • Improve the Bot and its features

We do not use collected information to sell personal data to third parties.

3. What We Do Not Collect

The Bot does not intentionally collect:

  • Discord passwords
  • Payment card information
  • Private messages unrelated to Bot functionality
  • The contents of voice conversations
  • Audio recordings of voice channels

4. Data Storage

Some information may be stored in databases or other systems used to operate the Bot.

The amount and type of stored information may vary depending on the features enabled by a Discord server.

Reasonable measures are used to protect stored information from unauthorized access. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent users' personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party infrastructure or service providers when necessary to operate the Bot, such as database, hosting, or logging services.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, protect the security of the Bot or its users, or investigate abuse, fraud, or unauthorized access.

6. Server Administrators

Server administrators may control whether the Bot is installed and which of its features are enabled.

If you do not want your activity processed by the Bot, you may leave a server that uses the Bot or contact the server's administrators regarding Bot usage.

7. Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of data associated with their Discord account where technically possible.

To request deletion, contact the Bot's developer through the official Tokyo After Dark Discord server.

When submitting a request, you may be asked to provide your Discord User ID so that the correct data can be identified.

Some information may need to be retained for legitimate technical, security, or legal purposes.

8. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Bot's features, maintain statistics, prevent abuse, resolve technical issues, or meet legal obligations.

Server-specific data may be deleted when the Bot is removed from a server, depending on the type of data and how the Bot is configured.

9. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

Users should not provide sensitive personal information to the Bot through commands or other interactions.

10. Third-Party Services

The Bot operates on Discord and may use third-party hosting, database, or other infrastructure providers.

Those services may process information necessary for the Bot to operate and may have their own privacy policies and terms.

Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own policies.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as the Bot's features or data practices change.

The "Last Updated" date will be changed when updates are made.

12. Contact

For questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, contact the Bot's developer through the official Tokyo After Dark Discord server.
